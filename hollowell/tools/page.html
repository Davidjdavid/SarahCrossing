How to play

You play a kid from a small valley town, setting out on a big day. The valley has more going on than it lets on: dungeons of hand-made rooms, tools that open up places you've already walked past, and secrets tucked into its corners.

The world runs on a few rules of its own. Push things around and watch what they do. Every puzzle follows from what you notice.

Every key and button can be changed in the game's menu, under Options, then Controls. The game's own hints follow whatever you set.

Screen

The Screen button switches between crisp pixels and two old-television looks. The same choice is in Options.

Saving

The game keeps your place whenever you go through a door, and you can save from the menu any time. There are three save slots. On this page your saves stay in this browser, and with your account when you're signed in, so you can carry on from another computer.

Click the game once so your keyboard reaches it. A controller works in this window when the browser lets the page read it; the line under the screen says if it can't. Sound starts with your first key or button press.
