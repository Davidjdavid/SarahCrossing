# ⚠️ SPOILERS — FULL WALKTHROUGH ⚠️

The intended route (the same one `tests/walkthrough.test.mjs` plays), then every secret.
Most puzzles have several solutions; these are just the obvious ones.

## Main route

1. **Home.** A crate weighing 9 blocks the door; you carry a 1. Stand beside it, press Z,
   swap: the crate weighs 1, you hold the 9. Push it aside, leave.
2. **Post office (No. 2).** The postmaster gives you the Keeper's letter ("No. 0, end of the
   Long Road") and mentions a parcel for No. 5 by the West Lane bridge.
3. **West Lane.** The parcel weighs 2, your strength is 1. Swap your 9 into your own
   strength. The bridge is age 9 (rotten): give it your 1. Push the parcel across and up into
   door No. 5. Visit Mrs. Fife: she tips you a **0** ("it's nothing").
   *Alternatives: relabel the parcel (its "to" number), or renumber a nearer door to 5.*
4. **Dot's (No. 3).** The satchel costs 300 and you have 12 gold. Put the 0 in the price's
   hundreds: free. Two pocket slots now.
5. **Wood Edge.** A slime (beat it: it leaves its 0 and 1). A tree of age 5 blocks the west
   path: give it a 1 (sprout). An old wall of age 6 blocks the north: give it 0 (never built)
   or 9 (rubble).
6. **Deep Wood.** The bridge is age 0, not built yet: give it anything and take its 0.
7. **Fort Gate.** The golem (health 99, strength 9) winds up ("!") when you step next to it:
   you get one free action. Swap a 0 into its strength, then beat it (its leftovers: 0 0 0 9).
8. **Fort Yard.** Cells 11–14; the armory was No. 15, bricked up. Get a 5 (the crate in cell 13
   weighs 5), make a cell door read 15, walk in: **spectacles** (hidden numbers appear).
9. **The Mere.** The sluice for the mill opens when its plate (need 6) is weighed down. A
   sapling of age 0 on the shore gives a 0. A boulder weighing 7 lies sunk in the channel:
   give it 0 and it floats up — step across on it, holding its 7. Push the islet's crate (1)
   onto the plate and swap the 7 into it.
10. **The Mill.** The wheel turns while the sluice is open; its counter counts up each turn.
    The strongbox opens at a hundred, and the hundreds drum is out of reach: put 9s in the
    tens/ones and let it carry (or just wait). **Pocket watch**: your own hour number.
11. **The Lighthouse (No. 0).** Put a 0 on any door (or walk/shorten the Long Road: its
    milestones are its length; 0 means you're there). The Keeper reads the letter: the lamp
    is the count's fifth digit and only lights when the count carries over.
    - **Ground floor:** the cat (weight 9) sits on the stairs; give it a light weight and it
      hops off. Its 9 can go in the first drum, or anywhere (the bell can roll that drum).
    - **Second floor:** a one-way ledge drops to the drum; the only way back is the door
      (address 4). Bring a 9 *and* a 0 down (satchel, or carry one in your own stats): drum
      gets the 9, the door gets the 0 → ground floor. The anchor (9) and cookie jar (0) help.
    - **Third floor:** a sun gate (open by day) and a moon gate (open at night). Pass the sun
      gate by day, use the watch to make it night, pass the moon gate, put a 9 in the drum.
      (Your own age, 08, lends a 0 for "0x" hours.)
    - **Fourth floor:** only with spectacles: the telescope's hidden 9 → the hidden top drum;
      the drum's old 0 → the newer-looking partition (age 1) → it was never built → stairs.
    - **The lamp room:** ring the bell. 9999 → 10000: the one is carried up into the lamp.
12. **Ending.** The cascade outside, the beam, a boat comes home. Credits; the island stays
    playable.

## Secrets (stamps; the album is in the post office)

| Stamp | How |
|------|-----|
| Hatchling | Deep Wood nook (wall age 7 → 0/9): give the egg age 1 or 2. The chick follows you everywhere. Age 3+ and it flies to its nest. |
| The Hollow | Deep Wood's old tree (age 8) → 9: it opens. A squirrel, a 6, the stamp. |
| Upside Down | Mirror Pond: the stone turns over whatever you put on it. Put a 6 on it, get a 9. |
| The Hour That Isn't | Set the town clock (or watch) to 24–99. Everyone vanishes (the guard too); the old clock tower on Clock Hill has a door. Inside: a third satchel slot. |
| Nowhere | Any door with an address nobody lives at (e.g. 9 before the lot is built, 47 on a fort door). The dead-letter office. |
| Housewarming | East Lane's lot is a house of age 0. Give it an age; visit No. 9. |
| The Old Dig | The Quarry's cracked floor holds 3. Be heavier (or push the 5-weight boulder in): fall into the cellar. Two 9s down there. |
| The Far Shore | With spectacles, the sea at the Harbor shows its width: ∞. Swap a 0 into it: the shore comes right up to the dock (a 1 leaves one row of sea between: cross it weighing nothing). You now carry ∞, which works almost everywhere (not in milestones: the road won't be endless). |
| Halfway | Walk the Long Road honestly (no milestone swaps) to its 50th screen. |

## Smaller things

- The guard on East Lane goes home at night, and steps aside if you make him a child
  (spectacles: everyone has an age).
- People react when you change their age; houses vanish at age 0 and crumble at 9 — and
  then nobody can get into the room behind their door number.
- Your age at 0: "You haven't been born yet." (undo).
- ∞ works everywhere: infinite health, strength, cookies; an endless road; a door to Nowhere.
- Dot notices when her prices change. Old Sev keeps a parcel addressed to him.
- The pause menu's "Walk home" gets you out of anywhere.
